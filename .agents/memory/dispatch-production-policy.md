name: Dispatch and production policy
description: User-confirmed dispatch targets and separate artwork requirements on production picksheets.
---

Publicly promise dispatch of all orders within 7 working days. Internally aim for 5 working days. The user confirmed both counts exclude weekends.

**Why:** The user wants the 5-day aim to remain internal while promising customers 7 days.

**How to apply:** Do not expose the 5-day target to customers or confuse dispatch with arrival. Do not silently count calendar days or introduce unconfirmed holiday rules.

Every order needs a production picksheet. Each product’s printed design must appear as a separate production item from the garment; “logo” here means the product’s printed design, not the Curvy Rails brand logo.

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified which artwork must be included separately for production.

**How to apply:** Order-linked picksheets must identify garments and their matching printed-design items, with the quantities needed for production.
