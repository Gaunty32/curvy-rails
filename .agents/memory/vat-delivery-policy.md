All prices on the website will show inclusive of VAT, including shipping.

UK mainland shipping is £3.99. Orders outside the UK mainland are still processed, but the shipping charge is confirmed separately.

Free shipping is available for orders over £50 on the UK mainland only. Shipping elsewhere is quoted separately, including for orders over £50.

**Why:** The user explicitly supplied these commercial rules.

**How to apply:** Do not add VAT on top of displayed prices or shipping. Do not reject non-mainland destinations, or imply their shipping is included when it must be confirmed separately. Preserve “over £50” rather than silently interpreting it as “£50 or more.” Apply free shipping only to UK mainland destinations. The shipping platform is not yet selected.
