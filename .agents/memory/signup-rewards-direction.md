**How to apply:** Keep optional fields optional in the screenshot-inspired Curvy Rails signup.

**Why:** The screenshot is a visual reference, not approval of its rewards policy.

**How to apply:** Do not copy the example’s earning rate, voucher values or 21-day waiting period. Keep unconfirmed rates and mystery offers visibly unconfirmed; do not present preview points or vouchers as live.
