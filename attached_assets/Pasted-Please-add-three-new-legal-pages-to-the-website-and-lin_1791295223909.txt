--------------------------------------------------

Create the following policy in clear, customer-friendly UK English.

RETURNS & REFUNDS

We hope you love your order, but if you change your mind you have the right to cancel and return most items purchased online.

14-DAY RIGHT TO CANCEL

You have 14 days from the day after you receive your order to tell us that you wish to cancel your order.

If an order contains multiple items delivered separately, the 14-day cancellation period starts from the day after you receive the final item.

You do not need to give us a reason for cancelling.

Once you have told us that you wish to cancel, you then have 14 days to return the goods to us.

HOW TO MAKE A RETURN

To request a return, contact us using the contact details shown on this website and provide:

- Your name
- Order number
- The item(s) you wish to return

We will then provide the appropriate return instructions and return address.

Please do not send goods to us without following the return instructions.

CONDITION OF RETURNED ITEMS

You may inspect and try on clothing in the same way that you reasonably would in a shop.

Returned items should, wherever possible:

- Be unworn other than for trying on
- Be unwashed
- Have their original tags attached
- Be returned with their original packaging where reasonably possible
- Be free from marks, odours, pet hair or other damage

If an item has been handled beyond what is reasonably necessary to establish its nature, characteristics and fit, and this has reduced its value, we may make an appropriate deduction from the refund where permitted by law.

RETURN POSTAGE

Unless the product is faulty, damaged, incorrect or not as described, you are responsible for the cost of returning the goods to us.

We recommend using a tracked postal service and keeping proof of postage until your refund has been completed.

If we have supplied an incorrect, faulty, damaged or misdescribed item, please contact us before returning it so that we can arrange the appropriate return method.

REFUNDS

Once we receive the returned goods, or you provide satisfactory evidence that they have been sent back, we will process the refund in accordance with your statutory rights.

Refunds will normally be made using the same payment method used for the original purchase.

Where you cancel the entire order under your statutory cancellation rights, we will also refund the cost of our standard delivery option. If you chose a more expensive delivery service, such as express delivery, we are not required to refund the additional delivery cost above our standard delivery charge.

PERSONALISED AND CUSTOM-MADE ITEMS

The statutory right to cancel does not normally apply to goods that have been made to your specification or clearly personalised.

This includes, for example:

- Clothing printed with a personalised name
- Clothing embroidered with a personalised name
- Bespoke wording or designs requested by the customer
- Items manufactured or altered specifically to the customer's individual requirements

This exclusion does not affect your rights where personalised goods are faulty, damaged, incorrectly supplied or not as described.

FAULTY GOODS

Your statutory rights are not affected by this returns policy.

If an item is faulty, damaged, not as described or otherwise does not meet the requirements of UK consumer law, please contact us as soon as possible.

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 or other applicable UK consumer legislation.
