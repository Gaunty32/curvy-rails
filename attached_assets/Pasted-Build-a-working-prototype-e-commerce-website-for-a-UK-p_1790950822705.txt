Build a working prototype e-commerce website for a UK plus-size clothing brand called Curvy Rails.

This is a demonstration site only. Do not connect live payments, Stripe, ShipStation, email sending or other production services yet.

Use the supplied Curvy Rails logo and recreate the visual direction of the approved homepage concept: predominantly black and white, bold, confident, modern, friendly and community-led. It must not look like a generic corporate e-commerce template.

Curvy Rails currently has an established community buying and selling pre-loved plus-size clothing through TikTok and Vinted. The new website represents the next stage: their own branded range of T-shirts and hoodies, initially sizes 16–32.

The primary objective of V1 is simple: sell products.
